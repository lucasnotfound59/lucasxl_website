## Research Question

How does confidence track correctness in humans and language models under a shared question format?

## Why It Matters

Reliable answers require more than accuracy: confidence should also reflect uncertainty.

## My Role

This overview introduces the study; a detailed contribution statement will be added with the public research materials.

## Methodology

The project uses matched judgment questions and confidence reports, with calibration and signal-detection analyses.

## Results and Evidence

The recorded work includes aggregate analysis and research communication materials. No participant-level data are included here.

## Limitations

Observed confidence behavior does not establish the existence of internal metacognition in a language model.
